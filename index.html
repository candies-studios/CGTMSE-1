    About CGTMSE

    Enabling Dreams. Supporting Enterprises.

    The Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises was set up jointly by the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises, Government of India, and the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) to catalyse the flow of institutional credit to micro and small enterprises.

    Through its Credit Guarantee Scheme, the Trust guarantees collateral-free and third-party-guarantee-free loans given by eligible Member Lending Institutions. The guarantee shares the lender's risk, which makes it easier for banks and NBFCs to lend to first-generation, under-served and new-to-credit entrepreneurs.

    Understanding the scheme

    How a CGTMSE guarantee works

    Three parties are involved. The entrepreneur borrows from a lender; CGTMSE stands behind the lender with a guarantee. You never apply to CGTMSE yourself.

    EntrepreneurMicro or small enterprise seeking a loan
    Loanwithout collateral
    Member Lending InstitutionBank, NBFC or financial institution registered with CGTMSE
    Guarantee coverfor a fee
    CGTMSEGuarantor to the lender, not a lender itself
    1. 1Entrepreneur

      Approach an eligible lender

      Apply for a term loan or working capital at a Member Lending Institution (bank, NBFC or FI) registered with CGTMSE.

    2. 2Lender

      Loan appraisal and sanction

      The lender evaluates the business and the proposal under its own credit appraisal, and sanctions or disburses the loan.

    3. 3Lender

      Lender applies for cover

      After sanction or disbursement, the lender lodges the guarantee application with CGTMSE online.

    4. 4CGTMSE

      Guarantee cover is issued

      CGTMSE approves the application and issues guarantee cover to the lender on receipt of the applicable fee, subject to scheme conditions.

    Important: CGTMSE does not grant loans, credit facilities or subsidies, and has no agents for arranging them. Talk to any Member Lending Institution directly.

    Who can benefit

    An ecosystem built around the small enterprise

    Women entrepreneurs

    Enhanced guarantee coverage for women-owned micro and small enterprises.

    Micro enterprises & manufacturing

    Workshops, artisans and small factories, new or existing.

    First-generation entrepreneurs

    No collateral or third-party guarantor needed to access formal credit.

    Small businesses

    Growing family-run enterprises.

    Service sector & trade

    Service activities under the MSMED Act, 2006, and retail and wholesale trade.

    Lending institutions

    Banks, NBFCs and FIs register as MLIs to share credit risk.

    MLI eligibility

    Eligibility as per the scheme FAQ: new and existing micro and small enterprises in manufacturing or services. Agriculture, Self Help Groups (SHGs) and Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) are not covered. Check eligible borrowers

    Measuring our impact

    Twenty-five years of widening access to credit

    From a new trust in 2000 to over 1.4 crore guarantees, CGTMSE has become a central part of how India's small enterprises get collateral-free finance.

    Dignitaries at the CGTMSE 25-year commemoration.

    Approving ₹1 lakh crore of guarantees in a single year, FY 2022-23, was a first for CGTMSE. By FY 2024-25 annual approvals had reached ₹3 lakh crore.Source: cgtmse.in introduction

    1. 2000Trust established

      Set up jointly by the Ministry of MSME and SIDBI to guarantee collateral-free loans to micro and small enterprises.

    2. FY 2022-23₹1 lakh crore in one year

      Annual guarantee approvals cross ₹1 lakh crore for the first time.

    3. 1 April 2023Scheme revamped

      Guarantee ceiling raised from ₹2 crore to ₹5 crore and fees reduced, following ₹9,000 crore of corpus infusion announced in Union Budgets 2022-23 and 2023-24.

    4. FY 2024-25₹3 lakh crore milestone

      Guarantees worth ₹3 lakh crore approved during the year.

    5. 1 April 2025Ceiling raised to ₹10 crore

      Higher cover per borrower, with a revised Annual Guarantee Fee structure.

    6. February 2026Export credit support

      Special Credit Guarantee Scheme for collateral support to MSME exporters.

    7. 31 March 20261.41 crore guarantees

      Cumulative approvals worth ₹13.67 lakh crore through 322 Member Lending Institutions.
